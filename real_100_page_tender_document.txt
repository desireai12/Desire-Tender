
    SECTION 1: NOTICE INVITING TENDER (NIT) & CONTRACTOR REGISTRATION
    Tender Reference No: NIT/WRD/GUJARAT/2026/8892
    Project Name: EPC Contract for Kankrej Water Supply & Solar Pumping Pipeline Network Project (Package IV)
    Department: Water Resources Department, Government of Gujarat

    CLAUSE 1.1: CONTRACTOR REGISTRATION CLASS
    The bidder must be registered in AA Class & Above with Gujarat State Water Resources Department (WRD) or Roads & Buildings (R&B) Department, or hold an equivalent enlistment in Class-I with Central Public Works Department (CPWD) or Public Health Engineering Department (PHED) of any Indian State Government.

    CLAUSE 1.2: ELECTRICAL CONTRACTOR LICENSE
    The main contractor or their designated electrical sub-contractor must hold a valid Class-A Electrical Contractor License issued by the Electrical Licensing Board of Gujarat, valid up to the date of tender submission.

    CLAUSE 1.3: ISO CERTIFICATIONS & QUALITY CONTROL
    The bidding entity must possess valid ISO 9001:2015 (Quality Management System) and ISO 14001:2015 (Environmental Management System) certifications applicable to water supply pipeline execution and solar infrastructure projects.

    CLAUSE 1.4: SITE VISIT CERTIFICATE
    Bidders must physically visit the project site at Kankrej Main Pumping Station, inspect the pipeline alignment (Package IV), and obtain a Site Visit Verification Certificate signed by the Executive Engineer, WRD Palanpur Division prior to bid submission.

    SECTION 2: FINANCIAL QUALIFICATION CRITERIA & BANKING REQUIREMENTS

    CLAUSE 2.1: AVERAGE ANNUAL FINANCIAL TURNOVER
    The bidder must have achieved an Average Annual Financial Turnover of not less than Rs. 69.78 Crores (Rupees Sixty-Nine Crores Seventy-Eight Lakhs Only) during the last 3 financial years (FY 2022-23, FY 2023-24, FY 2024-25), certified by a practicing Chartered Accountant with valid UDIN.

    CLAUSE 2.2: LIQUID ASSETS & CREDIT FACILITIES
    The bidder must demonstrate availability of Liquid Assets and/or Uncommitted Bank Credit Facilities of not less than Rs. 17.45 Crores (Rupees Seventeen Crores Forty-Five Lakhs Only) as on 31st March of the preceding financial year, supported by a Solvency Certificate / Credit Line Certificate issued by a Scheduled Commercial Bank.

    CLAUSE 2.3: FINANCIAL NET WORTH REQUIREMENT
    The Financial Net Worth of the bidder as on 31st March of the latest audited financial year must be positive and not less than Rs. 13.95 Crores (20% of estimated tender cost).

    CLAUSE 2.4: SOLVENCY CERTIFICATE
    Bidders must submit a Solvency Certificate of value not less than Rs. 20.00 Crores issued by a Scheduled Commercial Bank not older than 6 months prior to the date of tender opening.

    CLAUSE 2.5: PROFITABILITY CRITERIA
    The bidder must have earned net profit in at least two of the last three financial years. Certified audited balance sheets and profit & loss statements for the last 3 financial years must be attached.

    SECTION 3: TECHNICAL EXPERIENCE & SIMILAR WORK EXECUTION

    CLAUSE 3.1: SINGLE SIMILAR WORK EXPERIENCE
    The bidder as prime contractor must have successfully completed or substantially completed (at least 80% physical progress) at least ONE similar EPC water supply pipeline / pumping scheme project of value not less than Rs. 27.91 Crores in the last 7 financial years.

    CLAUSE 3.2: TWO SIMILAR WORKS EXPERIENCE (OPTION B)
    Alternatively, the bidder must have executed TWO similar EPC water pipeline projects, each costing not less than Rs. 17.45 Crores in the last 7 years.

    CLAUSE 3.3: THREE SIMILAR WORKS EXPERIENCE (OPTION C)
    Alternatively, the bidder must have executed THREE similar EPC water pipeline projects, each costing not less than Rs. 13.95 Crores in the last 7 years.

    CLAUSE 3.4: PIPELINE LENGTH AND DIAMETER EXPERIENCE
    Out of the executed projects under Clause 3.1 to 3.3, the bidder must have laid, jointed, and commissioned at least 15 km of High-Density Polyethylene (HDPE) or Ductile Iron (DI) pipeline of minimum diameter 600 mm in a single water supply project.

    CLAUSE 3.5: SOLAR POWER INFRASTRUCTURE EXPERIENCE
    The bidder or their technical partner must have installed and commissioned at least 1.5 MW Cumulative Grid-Connected / Off-Grid Solar PV Power System associated with water pumping stations in the last 5 years.

    CLAUSE 3.6: OPERATION & MAINTENANCE (O&M) EXPERIENCE
    The bidder must possess at least 3 years of successful Operation & Maintenance (O&M) experience for a rural/urban water supply pumping project serving a minimum design discharge of 20 MLD.

    SECTION 4: JOINT VENTURE (JV) / CONSORTIUM QUALIFICATION RULES

    CLAUSE 4.1: MAXIMUM NUMBER OF PARTNERS
    Joint Ventures are permitted with a maximum of TWO (2) partners (Lead Partner + One JV Partner).

    CLAUSE 4.2: LEAD PARTNER MINIMUM EQUITY & FINANCIAL SHARE
    The Lead Partner of the Joint Venture must hold a minimum equity share of 51% in the JV entity and must independently satisfy at least 50% of the Financial Turnover and Technical Experience requirements prescribed in Section 2 and Section 3.

    CLAUSE 4.3: OTHER JV PARTNER MINIMUM SHARE
    The other JV Partner must hold at least 25% share and satisfy at least 25% of the financial turnover requirement. All partners combined must meet 100% of all eligibility criteria.

    CLAUSE 4.4: TURNOVER & EXPERIENCE POOLING RULES
    Financial turnover, liquid assets, and single work experience values of both JV partners will be pooled strictly in proportion to their equity percentage in the Joint Venture Agreement.

    SECTION 5: TENDER SECURITY, BANK GUARANTEE & DEPOSITS

    CLAUSE 5.1: EARNEST MONEY DEPOSIT (EMD)
    The bidder must submit an Earnest Money Deposit (EMD) of Rs. 69.79 Lakhs (1% of estimated value) online via e-procurement portal or in the form of Bank Guarantee (BG) from a Scheduled Commercial Bank.

    CLAUSE 5.2: BANK GUARANTEE VALIDITY PERIOD
    The EMD Bank Guarantee must remain valid for a minimum period of 180 days from the date of technical bid opening.

    CLAUSE 5.3: PERFORMANCE BANK GUARANTEE (PBG)
    The successful bidder shall furnish a Performance Bank Guarantee (PBG) equal to 5% of the accepted contract value within 15 days of receiving the Letter of Award (LoA).

    CLAUSE 5.4: SECURITY DEPOSIT DEDUCTION
    A Security Deposit of 5% will be deducted from each running bill up to a total cap of 5% of the contract value, released after successful completion of the Defect Liability Period.

    CLAUSE 5.5: ADDITIONAL PERFORMANCE SECURITY FOR UNBALANCED BIDS
    If the quoted bid price is lower than 10% below the department's estimated cost, the bidder must submit an Additional Performance Security equal to the percentage difference below 10%.
